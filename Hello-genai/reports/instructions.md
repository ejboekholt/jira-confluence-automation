# How to Fill Out the Weekly Status Report

Use this template for a quick weekly update to project stakeholders and engineering leadership.

1. Fill in the overall project status under Executive Summary.
2. List team accomplishments and outcomes under Completed Work.
3. Record active work, team focus, and dependencies under In Progress.
4. Capture blockers, risk severity, and mitigation under Blockers and Risks.
5. Describe the next milestone or sprint focus under Next Week / Next Sprint.
6. Add manager-specific actions, escalations, or stakeholder notes under Manager Notes.
7. Keep each bullet concise and action-oriented.
8. Prefer facts and dates over general commentary.

Tip: This report works best when updated every Friday or at the end of each sprint cycle.
